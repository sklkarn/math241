    Section 5.2 Quizzes on Functions

    This section contains interactive quizzes and exercises for students to practice the concepts covered in the book. Each quiz includes multiple choice questions, open-ended questions, and exercises that require students to show their work. The quizzes are designed to reinforce understanding and provide immediate feedback on key topics such as solving inequalities, graphing functions, identifying asymptotes, and analyzing points of discontinuity. Students are encouraged to attempt each question and review the explanations provided for the correct answers to deepen their comprehension of the material.
