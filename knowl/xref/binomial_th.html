Binomial Theorem:
\begin{equation*} (a+b)^{n} = \left[ {}^{n}C_{0} a^n + {}^{n}C_{1} a^{n-1}b +{}^{n}C_{2} a^{n-2}b^2 +\cdots +{}^{n}C_{n} a^{n-n}b^n \right] \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} or, \, (a+b)^{n} = a^{n}+na^{n-1}b+\frac{n(n-1)a^{n-2}b^{2}}{2!}+\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)a^{n-3}b^{3}}{3!}+\cdots \end{equation*}
