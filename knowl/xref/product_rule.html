Product Rule: If \(f(x) = g(x) \cdot h(x)\) and both are differentiable, then
\begin{equation*} f'(x)=g'(x)h(x)+g(x)h'(x). \end{equation*}
or,
\begin{equation*} (uv)' = u'v + uv' \end{equation*}
or,
\begin{equation*} \frac{d}{dx}(uvw) = uv\frac{dw}{dx} + uw\frac{dv}{dx}+ vw\frac{du}{dx} \end{equation*}
