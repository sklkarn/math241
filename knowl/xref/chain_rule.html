Chain Rule: The chain rule allows differentiation of composite functions. If \(y = f(u)\) and \(u = g(x)\text{,}\) with \(f\) differentiable at \(u = g(x)\) and \(g\) differentiable at \(x\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy}{du}⋅\frac{du}{dx}. \end{equation*}
