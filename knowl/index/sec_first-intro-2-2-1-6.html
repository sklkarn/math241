Item 6.

Real Numbers: A real number is any rational or irrational number, including positive and negative whole numbers, integers, decimals, fractions, and numbers such as pi \((\pi)\) and Euler’s number \((e)\text{.}\)
🔗
Infinity \((\infty)\) and negative infinity \((-\infty)\) are not real numbers. Because infinity and negative infinity can have different values. For example, the set of whole numbers is infinite. So is the set of integers. But, the two sets are not of the same size.
🔗
