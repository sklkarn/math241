Example 2.3.2.

Evaluate:
\begin{equation*} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{(1-x)^{1/5}-1}{x} \end{equation*}
🔗
Solution.
Set, \(1-x=t^5\) and as \(x\to 0,\) \(t\to 1.\)
\begin{equation*} \lim_{t \to 1} \frac{(t-1)}{1-t^5} = - \lim_{t \to 1} \frac{\cancel{(t-1)}}{\cancel{(t-1)}(t^4+t^3+t^2+t+1)} =-\frac{1}{5}. \end{equation*}
🔗
Using Binomial Expression
\begin{equation*} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1-\frac{1}{5}x+\cdots -1}{x} = - \lim_{x \to 0}\frac{1}{5} \frac{x}{x} = -\frac{1}{5} \end{equation*}
🔗
Using L Hosppital’s Rule
\begin{equation*} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\frac{1}{5}(1-x)^{-4/5}(-1)}{1} = -\frac{1}{5}. \end{equation*}
