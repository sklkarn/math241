Summary of inequality rules in mathematical operation:
  1. When adding or subtracting \(+ve\) or \(-ve\) number on both sides of inequality there is no sign change of inequality. For example:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} 3x - 5 \lt 10 \, \Rightarrow \, 3x \lt 15 \end{equation*}
    and
    \begin{equation*} 2x + 5 \lt 10 \, \Rightarrow \, 2x \lt 5 \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. When multiplying or dividing \(+ve\) number on both sides of inequality there is no sign change of inequality. For example:
    \begin{align*} 3x \amp \lt 15 \\ or, \, x \amp \lt \frac{\cancelto{5}{15}}{\cancel{3}} \\ \therefore \, x \amp \lt 5 \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
  3. When multiplying or dividing \(-ve\) number on both sides of inequality there is sign change of inequality. For example:
    \begin{align*} -2x +4 \amp \gt 12 \\ or, \, -2x \amp \gt 8\\ x \amp \lt \frac{\cancelto{4}{8}}{-\cancel{2}} \\ \therefore \, x \amp \lt -4 \end{align*}
    Do not forget to flip sign, even if you are cross multiplying both side by \(-ve \) number.
    \begin{align*} -\frac{2}{x+3} \amp \gt -\frac{5}{x+3}; \, (x \gt -3)\\ -\frac{x+3}{2} \amp \lt -\frac{x+3}{5}\\ \frac{x+3}{2} \amp \gt \frac{x+3}{5} \end{align*}
    Hence, it is true as \(x+3 \gt 0; \, \forall_x \gt -3\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. When taking reciprocal of inequality having same \(+ve\) or \(-ve\) number on both sides, there is sign change of inequality. For example:
    \begin{align*} 10 \amp \gt 2 \\ or, \, \frac{1}{10} \amp \lt \frac{1}{2} \end{align*}
    or,
    \begin{align*} -10 \amp \lt -2 \\ or, \, -\frac{1}{10} \amp \gt -\frac{1}{2} \end{align*}
    However, When taking reciprocal of inequality having one side \(+ve\) and another side \(-ve\) number then, there is no sign change of inequality. For example:
    \begin{align*} 5 \amp \gt -2 \\ \frac{1}{5} \amp \gt -\frac{1}{2} \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
