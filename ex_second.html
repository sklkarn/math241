    32.

    Evaluate:
    \begin{equation*} \lim_{x\to 0} \frac{(e^{x^2} -1)\ln(1+\sin^2 x)}{(1-\cos 2 x)^2} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} = \lim_{x\to 0} \frac{(e^{x^2} -1)\ln(1+\sin^2 x)}{(2\sin 2 x)^2} \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} = \lim_{x\to 0} \frac{(e^{x^2} -1)}{(x)^2}\times \frac{\ln(1+\sin^2 x)}{\sin^2 x} \times \frac{x^2}{4\sin^2 x} = 1/4. \end{equation*}
    🔗

    39.

    Show that the function \(f(x) = \cos x^2\) is continuous.
    🔗
    Hint.
    Take a point \(x=c\) where the function is continuous. Then, at \(x=c,\) \(f(x)=f(c)=\cos c^2\)
    \begin{equation*} \lim_{x\to c^-} f(x)= \lim_{h\to 0} \cos (c-h)^2 =\cos c^2. \end{equation*}
    and
    \begin{equation*} \lim_{x\to c^+} f(x)= \lim_{h\to 0} \cos (c+h)^2 =\cos c^2. \end{equation*}
    i.e., the given function is continuous at \(x=c.\)
    🔗
