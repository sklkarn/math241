    1. Trigonometric Identities: in right angled triangle \(\vartriangle ABC,\)
      🔗
      1. \begin{equation*} h^{2}=p^{2}+b^{2} \end{equation*}
        🔗
      2. \begin{equation*} \sin\theta=\frac{p}{h}; \, \quad \cos\theta=\frac{b}{h}; \, \quad \tan\theta=\frac{p}{b} \end{equation*}
        🔗
      3. \begin{equation*} \sin\theta \csc\theta=1; \, \quad \cos\theta\sec\theta=1; \, \quad \tan\theta\cot\theta=1; \end{equation*}
        🔗
      4. \begin{equation*} \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=\tan\theta \end{equation*}
        🔗
        🔗
        • \begin{equation*} \sin2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta; \end{equation*}

        • \begin{equation*} \cos2\theta = \cos^{2}\theta-\sin^{2}\theta = 2\cos^{2}\theta-1 = 1-2\sin^{2}\theta =2\cos^{2}\theta-1. \end{equation*}
          🔗
          🔗
        🔗
        🔗
      6. If \(2\theta = \alpha,\) then
        • \begin{equation*} \sin\alpha=2\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\cdot\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}; \end{equation*}
          🔗
          🔗
        • \begin{equation*} \cos\alpha=1-2\sin^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2} = 2\cos^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}-1. \end{equation*}
          🔗
          🔗
        🔗
        🔗
      7. \begin{equation*} \sin(-\theta) = -\sin\theta; \, \quad \text{ and } \, \cos(-\theta) = \cos\theta; \end{equation*}
        🔗
      8. \begin{equation*} \sin(\theta \pm \frac{\pi}{2}) = \pm \cos\theta; \, \quad \text{ and } \, \cos(\theta \pm \frac{\pi}{2}) = \mp \sin\theta. \end{equation*}
        🔗
        🔗
        • \begin{equation*} \sin(\alpha\pm\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta\pm\cos\alpha\sin\beta; \end{equation*}

    2. Trigonometric Laws: in any \(\vartriangle A' B' C'\) with sides \(a, \,b,\) and \(c\) and angles \(\alpha,\,\beta,\) and \(\gamma\)
      🔗
      1. Law of sines:
        \begin{equation*} \frac{\sin\alpha}{a}=\frac{\sin\beta}{b}=\frac{\sin\gamma}{c} \end{equation*}
        🔗
      2. Law of cosines:
        \begin{equation*} c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}-2ab\cos\gamma \end{equation*}
